IoT Architecture

The physical robot is built using an ESP32 microcontroller. It uses ultrasonic sensors to navigate around obstacles and capacitive soil moisture sensors to check the soil's hydration level. When moisture drops below a threshold, the onboard water pump activates precisely at the plant's roots, conserving water.
